# Why the hardest game is the one they finish

SLOP3733 · Week 6

---

## A fact that ought to be strange

Souls games are the least accommodating mainstream games there are.

They kill you constantly, explain almost nothing, and take about a hundred
hours.

---

## And they sell to the people fighting games lost

A fighting game match is three minutes.

You can play one badly and still have fun.

Mechanically, it is the far easier thing to get into.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# The question is not why hard games sell

It is why the harder one **kept** the player the easier one lost

---

## Try to answer it without the word "difficulty"

Difficulty is what everybody notices, and it explains nothing.

Two games can be equally punishing and one of them empties the room.

---

## Measure the loop instead

Two numbers, for any activity:

- **time to failure** — from starting an attempt to finding out it didn't work
- **time to retry** — from that until you can try again

---

## A Souls boss

**90 seconds** to failure

**15 seconds** back to the fog gate

---

## A fighting game combo

**seconds** to failure in the lab

**days** until you find out whether it mattered in a real match

---

## Your assignment

**three weeks** to failure

no retry at all

---

## So the punishing one has the short loop

| Loop | to failure | to retry |
|---|---|---|
| Souls boss | 90 s | 15 s |
| Combo drill | seconds | days |
| Assignment | 3 weeks | never |

The cruelty is the disguise.

---

## The other half: how big is the lesson?

Ninety seconds is short enough that you only come out holding **one thing**.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> It always sweeps after the second swing.

Small. Cheap. Completely testable.

---

## And that is what does the work

You want to know **right now** whether that one thing is enough.

It usually isn't. So you learn a second thing.

Twenty attempts later you have twenty small true statements, and you win.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Curiosity is cheaper than discipline

At no point did you have to decide to be disciplined

---

## Two conditions that make it work

**Legible tells.** You lost because of something you can name — which is what
makes a lesson extractable at all.

**The boss does not change.** Between attempt 4 and attempt 20, the only
variable that moved is you.

---

## Most of your studying has neither

You cannot name why the last three hours failed.

And the material moves every time you look at it.
