This week you play, and the Lab is the playing. This hour is the recording
protocol, because the deaths are the data and undocumented deaths are not.

## One line per attempt

After every death, one line. Attempt number, then the sentence:

> This time I died because ______, so next time I will ______.

That is it. Fifteen seconds, written on the walk back, before you go through the
fog again. It has to be short enough to do on the walk back or you will stop
doing it around attempt six.

## Testable versus not

Most people's first ten lines are unfalsifiable and it is worth catching early.

- *I died because I was impatient* — not testable. There is no next attempt that
  confirms or denies it.
- *I died because I attacked after the second swing instead of rolling* —
  testable. One attempt settles it.

The difference is not effort, it is aim. The first sentence is about your
character; the second is about the fight. You already know you are impatient and
it has never once helped you.

This is the same distinction as week 3's: an observation about the machine beats
an opinion about yourself. It shows up again here because it is the single
transferable habit in the whole course.

## The moment it stops being random

Somewhere between attempt eight and attempt twenty-five, a fight flips. Before
the flip, deaths feel like things that happen to you. After it, deaths feel like
mistakes you made, and you can usually say which one before the death animation
finishes.

Mark that attempt in your log. Nothing about the boss changed at that point. The
flip is the arrival of a model, and the whole point of the attempt log is that
it makes the model visible while it is being built rather than only afterwards.

In week 8 you build the same thing for a subject, and knowing what the flip felt
like is how you will recognise it when it happens over a fortnight instead of an
hour.

## What counts

One boss, properly. Twenty-plus attempts on a single fight is worth far more
here than a tour of the first area. If you kill it in six, find a harder one —
a fight you beat before you needed a model teaches you nothing about models.

Bring the log to week 8.
