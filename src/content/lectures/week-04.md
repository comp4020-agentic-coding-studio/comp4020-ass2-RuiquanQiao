Here is the problem stated as narrowly as I can state it. You sit down for
thirty minutes. Perhaps fifteen of those minutes are real. The other fifteen are
not spent on your phone — they are worse than that. They are spent at the desk,
looking at the work, not advancing, in a slack and slightly unwilling state that
does not feel like rest and does not produce anything.

In a ranked match that state does not exist. Not because you are more
disciplined in a game, but because the game will not let thirty seconds pass
without telling you what the next thirty seconds are for.

So: build that, out of paper.

## The four parts

**A win condition, written before you start.** One sentence, and a stranger has
to be able to check it. "Work on the assignment" is not one. "Two body
paragraphs drafted, however bad" is. This is the single highest-value line on
the page and most people get it wrong for a fortnight.

**A wave clock.** Something short and recurring that forces a decision — five
minutes is a reasonable default. At each tick, one word: *continue*, *switch*,
or *stuck*. That is all. Its job is not to measure anything. Its job is to make
sure you cannot drift for eleven minutes without noticing.

**One scheduled objective.** A single contestable moment inside the block, set
in advance: at minute eighteen you will have the thing in a state you could show
someone. Objectives work in a MOBA because everyone can see the clock, and they
work here for the same reason — the ten minutes before one have a shape.

**A verdict.** At the end you write W or L. You decide, and you decide against
the win condition you wrote at the start, not against how the half hour felt.

## The anti-goal, and it is not optional

The paper version must be runnable in about eight seconds of writing. If your
sheet has a header, a mood field and a reflection box, you have built ceremony,
and ceremony is what gets abandoned in week two.

There is a stronger version of this warning that comes from having got it wrong:
be suspicious of any mechanism that would make you feel like a strange person
if someone walked in on you doing it. Study is already hard enough without also
having to perform being a fanatic about it. If it looks deranged, it will not
survive contact with your actual life, and it should not.
