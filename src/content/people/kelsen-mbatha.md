I climbed to Master twice on two different accounts, four years apart, and the
second climb took a third of the games. Nothing about my mechanics improved that
much in between. What changed is that the second time I knew, at every point in
a match, what the next ninety seconds were for.

That is the only thing I am trying to get across in weeks 2 to 5. A ranked match
is not engaging because it is exciting. It is engaging because it never leaves
you without a job. Wave states, camp timers, objective spawns — the game is a
machine for making sure you always know what you are supposed to be doing right
now, and the moment you don't, you feel it as loss.

Most study blocks are the opposite. You sit down for half an hour with nothing
telling you what the next ninety seconds are for, and the honest answer is that
about half of that half hour goes nowhere.

I mark the first assessment. I am not interested in how elaborate your mechanism
is. I want to watch you use it, and I want it to survive you being tired.
